# Texas-Election-Analysis
Texas 22-26 Governor race, ΔG method (Morissa Benson's locked formula, from the Nebraska Cedar County audit), residual math, forward-fit constants. Texas has no party registration: the Democratic share of Governor-primary voters stands in for registration share. 2026 figures are unofficial (Texas SOS canvass pending). Analytical model, not a claim of fraud.

- Wiki: https://github.com/mbenson8989/Texas-Election-Analysis/wiki
- Method origin: https://github.com/mbenson8989/Nebraska-Election-Analysis-/wiki/Cedar-Audit
- Data: Texas Secretary of State results (2022: results.texas-election.com; 2026: goelect.txelections.civixapps.com) and SOS early-voting turnout rosters; population from U.S. Census Vintage 2025.
- `TX_254_County_Governor_DeltaG.csv`: all 254 counties, largest to smallest population.
